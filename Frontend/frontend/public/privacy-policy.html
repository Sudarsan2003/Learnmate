LearnMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Terms of Use

In short:

  • LearnMate is used through schools. We collect only what a learning app needs: account details, the questions students ask, and quiz and game results.
  • A student's account is created only with a parent's or guardian's consent, given at sign-up or collected by the school.
  • Questions are answered by an AI service. We never sell personal data, show advertising, or build advertising profiles of children.
  • Parents, students and schools can ask to see, correct or delete data at any time.

1. Who we are

LearnMate (“we”, “us”) provides an AI study assistant, quizzes, learning games and progress reports to schools, their teachers and their students, through the website at srvlearnmate.in and the LearnMate mobile app. For the personal data described here, LearnMate is the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”). Each school that uses LearnMate also decides how LearnMate is used for its students, under a written agreement with us.

3. Children's data and parental consent

Most LearnMate users are under 18. Following section 9 of the DPDP Act:

  • A student account is created only after a parent or guardian agrees to this policy — by ticking the consent box at sign-up, or through the school, which collects consent from parents before giving us class lists.
  • Accounts that existed before we asked for consent are asked once, on their next sign-in, before they can continue.
  • We do not track children across other websites or apps, do not monitor their behaviour for advertising, and do not show them advertising of any kind.
  • We use children's data only to provide education to them through their school.

A parent or guardian may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us (section 10) or to the school. We will then stop processing the child's data and delete the account, except anything we must keep by law.

4. AI answers

When a student asks a question, the question — together with relevant passages from the school's uploaded books — is sent to our AI providers to produce an answer. Students should not type personal details such as phone numbers or home addresses into the chat. AI answers can be wrong; students should check important facts with their textbook or teacher.

Within a school, teachers and the school's administrators can see their students' quiz results, chat topics, progress and reports. Other schools cannot see them. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we disclose it to others only when the law requires us to.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account details and learning records: while the account is active and the school uses LearnMate.
  • Chat history: until the student deletes it in the app, or the account is deleted.
  • When a school stops using LearnMate, we delete its students' data within 90 days, unless the school asks us to hand it back first.
  • Sign-in security records are kept only as long as needed to protect accounts.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, sign-in is paused after repeated wrong passwords, sessions end automatically, and each school's data is kept separate. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting personal data happens, we will inform the affected schools, users and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Students, and parents or guardians on their behalf, can:

  • ask what personal data we hold and who we have shared it with;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if they are unable to;
  • complain to us (section 10), and if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Account and data deletion

LearnMate accounts are managed by schools. To delete a student's account, contact the school's LearnMate administrator, who can delete it from Manage users, or write to us directly (section 10). Deleting an account removes its personal data, chats, quiz results and game progress, except anything we must keep by law. Students can also delete individual chats or their whole chat history in the app at any time.

10. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests about your data, or complaints, write to our Grievance Officer at annalurusudarsan@gmail.com. Please include the student's username and school. We will respond promptly, and in any case within the time the law requires.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and ask users (or parents) to agree again in the app before continuing.
