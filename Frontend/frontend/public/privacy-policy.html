LearnMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

LearnMate is an educational learning platform that provides students and educators with learning assistance, educational content, quizzes, games, document-based learning, and AI-powered study support.

This Privacy Policy explains what information LearnMate may collect, how that information is used, and the choices available to users of the LearnMate website and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LearnMate, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Username and account credentials.
  • Email address, when provided.
  • Mobile number, when provided.
  • Gender, when provided.
  • Address, when provided.
  • Institution or school information.
  • Standard, class, or educational level.
  • Authentication and account-role information.
  • Chat messages and chat history.
  • Quiz attempts, answers, scores, and results.
  • Game progress, scores, stars, levels, and related results.
  • Educational documents uploaded through LearnMate.

2. Account Information

When you create a LearnMate account, you may provide information such as a username, password, email address, mobile number, gender, address, institution, and standard or class.

This information is used to create and manage your account and to provide features associated with your LearnMate profile.

3. Authentication and Session Information

LearnMate uses authentication tokens to maintain your signed-in session. On the mobile application, authentication session information is stored using the device's secure storage facilities. On the web application, authentication information is stored locally in the browser.

4. AI Chat and Learning Assistance

LearnMate provides AI-powered educational assistance. Questions and other information submitted through the chat functionality are sent to the LearnMate backend so that the requested learning functionality can be provided.

Chat history and chat sessions may be stored so that users can review previous conversations and continue their learning sessions.

Users can use the available chat-history controls to delete chat history or individual chat sessions where those controls are provided.

5. Quizzes and Educational Activities

LearnMate processes information associated with quizzes and educational activities, including quiz participation, answers, results, scores, and related progress.

LearnMate may also provide leaderboards or similar educational features based on activity results.

6. Games and Learning Progress

LearnMate includes educational games. Information such as game progress, levels, scores, stars, streaks, and other game results may be processed to provide these features.

Some leaderboard features may use institution-related information to determine which users participate in an institution-specific leaderboard.

7. Educational Documents

LearnMate may allow authorized users to upload educational documents for learning and document-based educational functionality.

Documents submitted through LearnMate are transmitted to the LearnMate backend for processing and providing the requested functionality.

8. How We Use Information

Information processed by LearnMate may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide AI-powered learning assistance.
  • Maintain chat sessions and learning history.
  • Provide quizzes and educational activities.
  • Track educational game progress.
  • Provide educational documents and document-based functionality.
  • Provide leaderboards and related learning features.
  • Maintain and operate the LearnMate service.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service.

9. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

LearnMate sends information to its backend services when required to provide application functionality.

The specific third-party services or AI providers used to process information may depend on the current LearnMate backend configuration. Users should review this section again whenever the service architecture or service providers change.

10. Data Security

LearnMate uses authentication mechanisms and secure communication between the application and its backend services. Mobile authentication information is stored using secure device storage.

While reasonable security measures are used to protect information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

LearnMate retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain accounts and learning functionality, and satisfy applicable operational or legal requirements.

Specific retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

12. Account and Data Deletion

LearnMate is a school-managed educational platform. Student accounts are managed by the school or educational institution so that authorized school administrators can manage accounts and review student learning progress.

If a student wants their LearnMate account deleted, they must contact their school or institution administrator directly. The authorized school administrator is responsible for reviewing and processing the student's account deletion request.

Students can use this Privacy Policy as the web resource for account deletion information. The deletion request should be initiated by contacting the student's school or institution administrator.

LearnMate administrators can remove student accounts from the system. When an account is deleted, associated personal information and account-related data will be deleted, except where certain information must or may be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud prevention, or other applicable requirements.

Because LearnMate accounts are managed by schools or educational institutions, students do not have a self-service account deletion function inside LearnMate. Students should contact their school or institution administrator to initiate an account deletion request.

13. Children's Privacy

LearnMate is an educational platform intended to support students and educators through school or educational institution-managed accounts.

Where LearnMate is used by students who are children or minors, the school or educational institution is responsible for managing student accounts in accordance with its applicable policies and requirements.

Parents or guardians who have questions about a student's LearnMate account should contact the student's school or educational institution administrator.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when LearnMate's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
